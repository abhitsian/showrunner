title: Discovery call

# Open (3m)

## [talk] Agenda and goal
- Thanks, agenda, and what a good outcome looks like today.
- Confirm time: we have 30 minutes?

# Discovery (15m)

## [talk] Questions
> Tick through these; don't read them as a list.

- How do employees get help today?
- What breaks most often?
- Who owns the employee experience?
- What have you tried, and why didn't it stick?
- What would success look like in six months?

# Show (8m)

## [demo] The part that answers their pain
@chrome demo\.example\.com
Show only what maps to what they said. Skip the rest.

# Next steps (4m)

## [talk] Close
- Summarise what you heard, in their words.
- Propose the next step with a date.

# Q&A

## Pricing?
Give the range for their size, then offer a follow-up with the account team.

## Integration with our tools?
Name the connectors you have; offer a technical session for the rest.
